Where does the Fermi level sit?

Scroll to follow the story, then take over the controls.

Pure silicon

With no dopants, electrons and holes are equal in number, so the Fermi level sits near the middle of the gap.

Add donors

Each phosphorus atom donates an electron. As the doping rises, the Fermi level climbs toward the conduction band.

Swap to acceptors

Boron grabs electrons and leaves holes behind. Now the Fermi level drops below the middle of the gap.

Turn up the heat

Heat creates electron–hole pairs from the crystal itself. At high temperature they swamp the dopants, and the Fermi level drifts back to the middle.
